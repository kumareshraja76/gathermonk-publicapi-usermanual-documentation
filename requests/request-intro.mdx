# Part A: Requests — Overview & Introducing to Customers

> **Everything you need to know about the Requests module: what it is, why organisations use it, and how to introduce the document collection experience to your clients.**

---

## What is Requests?

The **Requests** module is GatherMonk's flagship solution for structured document and data collection. It replaces disorganized email threads, insecure attachments, and messy shared folders with a centralized, professional workspace.

With Requests, you define exactly what documents or data points you need from one or thousands of recipients, track submission progress in real time, and review submissions field-by-field.

## How to Introduce Requests to Your Customers & Clients

When asking clients, vendors, or external partners to submit sensitive documents, clarity, ease-of-use, and trust are essential. Here is how to present the GatherMonk experience to your recipients:

### 1. Zero Friction — No Account or Password Required

- External clients do **not** need to register, download an app, or remember another password.
- They simply click the unique, secure link in their email invitation to access their personalized submission page in any web browser.

### 2. Clarity on Exactly What is Needed

- Each request clearly outlines the requested documents (e.g., photo ID, tax forms, proof of address, certificates) along with instructions, accepted file formats, and file size limits.
- Visual indicators show what is **Required** vs. **Optional**, preventing incomplete submissions.

![Client-side submission experience — no login required](/help-guide/img_p29_1.png)

### 3. Field-by-Field Review — No Redoing Everything

- If an uploaded document is incorrect (e.g., a blurry photo ID or expired license), you can approve all valid items and reject _only_ the specific file needing changes with an inline note.
- The recipient receives a clear email notification and only has to resubmit that single item — eliminating the frustration of starting from scratch.

### 4. Automatic, Polite Reminders

- GatherMonk sends automated reminder emails (e.g. at 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before the deadline) so you don't have to spend hours manually following up.

### 5. Branded & Trustworthy

- The submission portal features your organization's logo, brand colors, and custom header, giving recipients immediate confidence that their sensitive data is going to a legitimate, trusted organization.

### 6. Bank-Grade Security & Privacy

- Client documents and data are encrypted in transit and at rest, ensuring full compliance with privacy regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2).

## The Request Lifecycle at a Glance

1. **Build or Choose Template**: Select from 4,000+ pre-built industry templates or create custom fields (text, files, checkboxes, dates, signatures).
2. **Assign Recipients**: Add contacts individually or import hundreds via CSV.
3. **Send & Track**: Recipients get email invites; you track real-time status (`Draft`, `Sent`, `Partially Submitted`, `Submitted`, `Completed`, `Overdue`).
4. **Review & Approve**: Approve or reject individual items with inline comments.
5. **Export & Complete**: Download all approved documents in a single organized ZIP package or sync to your CRM.
