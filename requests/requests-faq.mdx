# Frequently Asked Questions — Requests

- **Do recipients need to install software or register an account?**  
  No. Recipients open their unique submission link in any browser on any device. No login, account, or app is required.

- **Can I modify a template before sending?**  
  Yes. GatherMonk includes 4,000+ ready-made templates filterable by industry, category, type, and role. Select any template in Step 1 and it pre-populates the builder with relevant fields. You can modify everything after selecting it.

- **How are reminder emails handled?**  
  GatherMonk sends reminder emails automatically to any recipient who has not yet submitted, based on the Communication Schedule you assign in the Finalize step. Once a recipient submits, they are removed from the reminder queue.

- **Can recipients modify an approved item?**  
  No. Once you approve an item it is permanently locked. The recipient cannot modify, re-upload, or overwrite it.

- **How do I download all submitted files?**  
  Use the one-click **ZIP export** from the request dashboard. GatherMonk packages all approved files into a single organised archive.
