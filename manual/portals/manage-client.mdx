# Chapter 18: Managing Clients

The **Clients** tab inside a portal lists every client who has been invited, their email address, access status, and last login date.

![Client list — invited clients and access status](/manual/help-guide/img_p52_1.png)

---

### Adding a New Client

Click **Add Client** and enter the client's email address and name. GatherMonk sends them an invitation email with their unique portal access link.

> 💡 **Tip:** Clients only ever see content assigned specifically to them — even within a shared portal subdomain.
