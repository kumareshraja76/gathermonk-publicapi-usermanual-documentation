# Part D: Client Portal — Overview & Introducing to Customers

> **Everything you need to know about Client Portals: dedicated, branded workspaces where clients access requests, guides, and transfers with zero-friction login.**

---

## What is Client Portal?

The **Client Portal** gives each of your clients a dedicated, branded workspace. Instead of searching through multiple emails and links, clients log into their own secure portal where they see all their active requests, submitted documents, informational guides, and transfers in one unified hub.

![Portals Dashboard](/manual/help-guide/img_p52_1.png)

---

## How to Introduce the Client Portal to Your Customers

### 1. Magic Link / OTP Login — No Passwords

Clients log in using a one-time passcode sent directly to their email — no need to create or remember a password.

### 2. Everything in One Central Location

Clients can track what documents are pending, view approved submissions, download delivered files, and read instructional guides.

### 3. Fully White-Labeled & Branded

The portal is customized with your organization's logo, colors, and welcome message for a truly high-end client experience.
