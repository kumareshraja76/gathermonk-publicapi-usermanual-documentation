# Chapter 22: Client Login Experience

Clients access the portal using GatherMonk's **OTP (one-time passcode)** login — no password required.

---

### The Login Page

Clients land on a branded login page showing the portal name on the left and an email entry field on the right. They enter their email address and click Send Login Code.

![Client portal login — branded login page with OTP email entry](/manual/help-guide/img_p61_1.png)

---

### My Requests & Guides View

After logging in, clients see their **My Requests** tab listing all document or data collection requests assigned to them.

![Client portal — My Requests tab showing assigned requests](/manual/help-guide/img_p62_1.png)
