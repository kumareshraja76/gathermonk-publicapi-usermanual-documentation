# Chapter 5: Reviewing & Approving Submissions

The **Approval & Rejection workflow** is one of GatherMonk's most powerful differentiators. Unlike a standard form where you simply receive answers, GatherMonk lets you review every submitted file and answer individually — approving what is correct and rejecting only what needs to change, without asking the recipient to redo everything from scratch.

---

### The Four-Stage Review Workflow

1. **Submission & Notification** — Instant notification when recipient submits.
2. **Item-Level Review** — Review and approve or reject each item independently.
3. **Client Correction** — Rejected items highlight feedback for resubmission. Approved items stay locked.
4. **Export & Completion** — One-click ZIP download of all approved files.

![Reviewing a submitted response — field-by-field approval and rejection](/manual/help-guide/img_p26_1.png)

---

### Approving an Item

Click **Approve** on any field or file to accept it. Approval permanently locks that item so the recipient cannot overwrite it.

---

### Rejecting an Item

Click **Reject** on any field or file and write an inline comment explaining exactly what needs to change. Your comment is pinned directly to that specific field or file.

---

### Audit Trail

Every action — approval, rejection, comment, and resubmission — is permanently recorded with the reviewer's name and a timestamp.
