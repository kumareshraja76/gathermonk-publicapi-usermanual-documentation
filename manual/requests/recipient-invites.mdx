# Chapter 3: Managing Recipients

GatherMonk supports unlimited recipients per request. Each recipient receives a personalised, trackable submission link.

---

### Adding a Client to a Request

From the request dashboard, click **Add Client**. Enter the recipient's email address and name. Each recipient is sent an individual invitation containing their unique submission link.

![Adding a client to a request](/manual/help-guide/img_p16_1.png)

---

### Sending Invitations

After adding a client, send the invitation email from within GatherMonk. You can customise the invitation message using the pre-made email template library, or write a custom message.

![Sending an invitation to a recipient](/manual/help-guide/img_p17_1.png)

---

### Removing an Invitation

To remove a recipient from a request, open the invitation record and select **Delete**. This revokes their submission link and removes them from tracking.

![Deleting an invitation](/manual/help-guide/img_p18_1.png)

---

### Automated Reminders

GatherMonk tracks every recipient who has not yet submitted and sends follow-up emails on a schedule you define (e.g. 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before due date). Recipients who have completed their submission are never chased.
