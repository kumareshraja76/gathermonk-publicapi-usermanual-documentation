# Chapter 7: Client-Side Experience

When a recipient receives their invitation email and clicks their unique submission link, they see the GatherMonk request form in their browser — **no login or account required**.

![Client-side request form — what recipients see when they open their submission link](/manual/help-guide/img_p29_1.png)

The form shows the header title, description, and all fields in the order defined in the Builder. Recipients fill in text fields, select dropdown options, answer questions, and upload files directly from this page. When all required fields are complete, they click **Submit**.

After submitting, recipients see a confirmation message. If any fields are later rejected by the reviewer, they receive an email notification and can return to the form to resubmit only the rejected items.
