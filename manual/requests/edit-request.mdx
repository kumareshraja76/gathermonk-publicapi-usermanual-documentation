# Chapter 4: Editing a Live Request

After a request is published, you can update its status, due date, communication template, and visibility without re-sending it to recipients.

---

### Changing Status

Click the **Status** option on the request and choose from the available states (e.g. _Active_, _Paused_, _Closed_). Closing a request stops recipients from submitting new responses.

![Editing request status](/manual/help-guide/img_p19_1.png)

![Selecting a new status](/manual/help-guide/img_p20_1.png)

---

### Changing Due Date

Click **Due Date** to open the date picker and select a new deadline. Recipients will receive updated reminder emails reflecting the new date.

![Editing the due date](/manual/help-guide/img_p21_1.png)

---

### Changing Communication Template

The communication template controls the email style used for invitation and reminder messages sent to recipients.

![Editing the communication template](/manual/help-guide/img_p22_1.png)

---

### Changing Visibility

Visibility controls who can see the request within your organisation (Public, Private, or Password Protected).

![Editing visibility](/manual/help-guide/img_p24_1.png)
