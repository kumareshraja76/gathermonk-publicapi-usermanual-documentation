# Part B: Forms — Overview & Introducing to Customers

> **Everything you need to know about the Forms module: what it is, how to build interactive questionnaires, and how to introduce forms to your respondents.**

---

## What is Forms?

The **Forms** module gives you an easy-to-use drag-and-drop form builder to collect answers, survey feedback, lead information, and event registrations. Unlike basic form tools, GatherMonk Forms connects directly to your contacts database and integrates into the same unified ecosystem as Requests and Client Portals.

![Forms Dashboard](/help-guide/img_p32_1.png)

---

## How to Introduce Forms to Your Customers & Respondents

### 1. Instant Access — Zero Logins

Respondents can open your form from any device — mobile, tablet, or desktop — without needing to create an account or sign into Google.

### 2. Beautiful & Branded Experience

Your forms look polished and modern, matching your brand colors and featuring your company logo.

### 3. Flexible Sharing Options

Share forms via direct link, embed them seamlessly onto your website, or print a QR code for on-site events.

### 4. Fast & Mobile-Friendly

Designed to look stunning on mobile screens with responsive inputs and smooth validation.
