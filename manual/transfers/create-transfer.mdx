# Chapter 13: Creating a Transfer

Click **Create new transfer** to open the transfer creation form.

![Create Transfer — transfer name, files, message, recipients, and security settings](/manual/help-guide/img_p41_1.png)

Fill in the following fields:

- **Transfer Name** — an internal label to identify this transfer
- **Files** — drag and drop files or click to upload. Supports all file types; up to 15 files at 15 MB each. Your remaining storage is shown in the sidebar.
- **Message to recipients (optional)** — a personalised note displayed to the recipient alongside the download link

---

### Choosing a Contact

Type an email address directly or click the contact book icon to select from saved contacts.

![Choosing a contact from the address book](/manual/help-guide/img_p42_1.png)

---

### Linking to a Client Portal

Toggle **Show in client portal** to on, then select the portal where this transfer should appear. Clients logged into that portal will see the transfer in their Transfers tab.

![Linking the transfer to a Client Portal](/manual/help-guide/img_p43_1.png)

---

### Security & Expiry Settings

- **Password protection** — require recipients to enter a password before accessing the files
- **Set expiry date** — set a date after which the download link becomes inactive
- **Notify me on download** — receive an email notification each time a recipient downloads the files
