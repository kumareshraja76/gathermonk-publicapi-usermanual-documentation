# Part C: Transfers — Overview & Introducing to Customers

> **Everything you need to know about the Transfers module: secure, branded file sending with password protection, expiry dates, and portal linking.**

---

## What is Transfers?

The **Transfers** module is GatherMonk's secure, high-capacity file delivery feature. When you need to send large deliverables, project assets, contracts, or media packs to clients or vendors, Transfers provides a secure alternative to untracked email attachments or third-party transfer sites.

![Transfers Dashboard](/manual/help-guide/img_p44_1.png)

---

## How to Introduce Transfers to Your Customers

### 1. Direct Download Without an Account

Recipients receive an email with a secure link and can download their files immediately.

### 2. Password Protection & Security

Sensitive transfers can be locked with a custom password for maximum confidentiality.

### 3. Expiry Dates & Access Controls

Set transfers to expire after 7, 14, or 30 days to ensure stale files do not remain accessible.

### 4. Integrated with Client Portals

Transfers can be linked directly to client portals so all files remain organized in one place.
