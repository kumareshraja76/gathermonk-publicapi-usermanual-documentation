# Frequently Asked Questions — Transfers

- **What are the file size and transfer limits?**  
  Up to 15 files per transfer, with a maximum of 15 MB per file. Your total storage allowance (5 GB on the standard plan) is shown in the transfer creation panel.

- **What file formats are supported?**  
  All file types are supported — documents, images, videos, archives, design files, and more. There are no restrictions on file format.

- **Do recipients need an account to download files?**  
  No. Recipients click their download link in the email notification and access the files directly in their browser. If you enabled password protection, they enter the password first.

- **What happens when a transfer link expires?**  
  The download link becomes inactive and recipients see an "expired" message. The files remain in your GatherMonk storage — you can edit the transfer and extend the expiry date to reactivate the link.

- **What is the difference between a Transfer and a Request?**  
  A **Transfer** is for sending files to someone — you are the sender, they are the downloader. A **Request** is for collecting files from someone — they upload, you review and approve.
