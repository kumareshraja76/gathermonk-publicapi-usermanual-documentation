# Frequently Asked Questions — Client Portal

- **Do clients need to remember passwords?**  
  No. Clients log in using a one-time passcode (OTP) sent to their email — no account creation, no password, no app download required. Enterprise clients can use SSO / SAML instead.

- **Are clients able to see other clients' information?**  
  Clients see only their own content — the Requests assigned to them, any Transfers sent to them, Guides and instruction pages, and the portal FAQ library. They never see other clients' data.

- **Can I white-label the portal with my own custom domain?**  
  Yes, on the Business plan. Business plan portals support full white-label removal of GatherMonk branding, custom domain mapping (e.g. `portal.yourcompany.com`), and complete logo and colour customisation.

- **What is the FAQ Manager inside the portal?**  
  The FAQ Manager lets you build a searchable FAQ library inside each portal. It tracks what clients search for, surfacing content gaps automatically.
