# Appendix

---

### A — Glossary

- **Request** — A structured form sent to one or more recipients for document or data collection. Tracked in real time with field-by-field approval.
- **Recipient** — An external contact who receives a request invitation and submits files or answers.
- **Form** — A standalone digital form for capturing structured data (answers, not files). Shared publicly via link, QR, or embed.
- **Transfer** — A secure file delivery from you to a recipient. Supports password protection, expiry dates, and client portal linking.
- **Client Portal** — A branded, authenticated workspace where a specific client sees all their requests, tasks, guides, and transfers in one place.
- **Guide** — A rich-text instruction page published inside a portal to help clients understand what is required of them.
- **OTP / Magic Link** — One-time passcode login — clients enter their email and receive a single-use login code. No password required.
- **Approval** — An admin's decision to accept a submitted field or file.

---

### B — Tips for High-Volume Use

- **Use templates** — save any well-built request as a template to re-use for recurring workflows.
- **Bulk invite recipients** — import a CSV of recipient emails rather than adding them one by one.
- **Set aggressive reminders** — configure reminders at 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before the deadline.
- **Create one portal per client** — individual portals keep each client's data private and make the portal experience feel personal.
- **Link transfers to portals** — use Transfers with "Show in client portal" enabled so clients have a single place to find everything.
- **Pre-write Guides** — create Guides before sending requests to reduce repetitive support questions.
