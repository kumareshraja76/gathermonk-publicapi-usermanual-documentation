# Create an Form

Follow these steps to create a new Form in GatherMonk.

## Step 1: Open Forms

From the GatherMonk dashboard, select **Forms** from the main navigation.

## Step 2: Create a New Form

Click **Create Form**.

Enter the required Form information:

- Form name
- Field name
- Field description

## Step 3: Configure the Form

Configure the Form settings according to your requirements.

## Step 4: Save

Click **Create Form** to save the Form.

Your new Form will appear in the Forms list.

<Tip>
  Make sure the Form date and time are correct before publishing the Form.
</Tip>
