# Frequently Asked Questions — Forms

- **Do respondents need an account?**  
  No. Anyone with the link or QR code can submit without creating an account, downloading an app, or logging in.

- **Can I export form responses to Excel or CSV?**  
  From the Forms dashboard, open any form and go to the Responses tab. Filter responses by date or field value, then click Export to download as CSV or Excel. There are no limits on exports across any plan.

- **Are there limits on how many responses I can receive?**  
  No. All GatherMonk plans support unlimited form responses.
