# Chapter 11: Client-Side Form Experience

When a respondent opens the form link, they see a clean form interface in their browser — **no login required**.

Respondents fill in all fields and click **Submit**. Their response is immediately recorded in the Forms dashboard and you receive a notification. All responses are stored with a timestamp and the respondent's email address (if captured as a field).

> ℹ️ **Note:** Forms can be filled in on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile.
